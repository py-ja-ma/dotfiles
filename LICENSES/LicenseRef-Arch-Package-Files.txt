LicenseRef-Arch-Package-Files

The files under hosts/<host>/etc/ are copies of configuration files from a host's /etc,
taken by `dram harvest`. Most of them are files an Arch Linux package installs (pacman,
mkinitcpio, glibc's locale.gen and others), with the host's local edits. Each keeps the
license of the package that ships it; the local edits are offered under the same terms.
The files a host writes itself (fstab, hostname, vconsole.conf) hold facts about that host.
