LicenseRef-Arch-Recipe

The package recipes under pkgs/<name>/ (PKGBUILD, .SRCINFO and the helper files a
PKGBUILD installs) were copied from the Arch Linux package sources or the Arch User
Repository (AUR), then reviewed and pinned. Each recipe keeps the terms its source
repository grants, and its maintainers and contributors are named in the header of its
PKGBUILD. The changes made here (pins, removed steps, added checks) are offered under the
same terms as the recipe they change.

The software a recipe builds is under its own license, which the PKGBUILD's license=()
array names; nothing built is part of this repository.
