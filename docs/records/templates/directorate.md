# Directorate page

[Office of Records](../README.md) › [Templates](README.md) › Directorate page

A directorate page fits on one screen.
It indexes its divisions and states the rules that hold across them; steps belong in the handbook or a branch page.

````markdown
# <Directorate>

[Organization](../README.md) › <Directorate>

<One paragraph: what this directorate is responsible for, on which machines, and the rule it lives by.>

| Division | Covers |
|---|---|
| [<Division>](<slug>/README.md) | <a few nouns> |

## Rules

<Up to five rules that hold across the divisions.
Each names the decision record behind it and the check that enforces it.>

## Decisions

<The records and log entries this directorate owns: link the rows of decisions/README.md#by-division.>

## Open

<Dated items that aren't done. Nothing else.>
````
