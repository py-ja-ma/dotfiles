## Known, accepted

- `Alt+Shift` fires on the Shift press, so an app chord that starts Alt→Shift (Alt+Shift+Tab) will also flip the layout; `SUPER+ALT+SPACE` is the clean alternative. Right Alt/Shift do nothing.
- The clock tooltip heading is `октября 2026` (genitive); waybar's calendar formatter cannot produce the nominative `октябрь`.
- `custom/updates` caches for 30 minutes, so it can disagree with the welcome toast's live count for up to that long.
- `dram backup check` honours borgmatic's cadence (2 weeks); `borgmatic check --force` forces it.
